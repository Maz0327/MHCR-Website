About us

We remodel kitchens, bathrooms and basements around Denver. We also put in hardwood and tile floors, finish fireplace walls and take on plenty more. Every job, big or small, gets the same precision and care.

From the owner

Over 18 years ago, my parents hired a company to finish the basement in their home. I was very involved and hands-on throughout that project, and I developed a strong passion for bringing visions to life. From that moment, I knew this was the industry for me.

Hundreds of projects later, the same passion still burns inside me. With the experience and knowledge I've gained over the years, I still bring the same hands-on approach to every job.

Every project is different, so we work closely with you, the homeowner, to understand your wants and needs for your home. We custom build each and every phase of your project. We take a great deal of pride in our work and never take shortcuts, so you're left with a finished product you can feel proud of. My goal is to leave a lasting impression on you and your home that keeps a smile on your face.

My motto is "If you're going to do something, do it right or don't do it at all." We always choose to do things right.

Omar, owner
