Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers Mile High Custom Remodels ("we," "us") and this website. If you have a question about it, call us at (720) 492-4326.

What we collect

  • When you send a request: your name, your phone number, your email address if you give it, the kind of work you need and anything you write in the message.
  • When you call us: your phone number and what you tell us about the job.
  • When you visit the site: the company that hosts this site keeps standard server logs. Those include your IP address, your browser type and the pages you open.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

This site doesn't use analytics, ad tracking or cookies of its own.

How we use it

We use your information to call you back, talk through the job and write your quote. If you hire us, we use it to schedule the work, do the work and bill you. We won't send you marketing messages unless you agree to them.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • the form service that delivers your request to us;
  • the trades and suppliers working on your job, and only what they need to do their part;
  • anyone the law requires us to share it with, or when we need to protect our legal rights.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or email system is completely secure, though, so we can't promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you. You can also ask us to correct it or delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you. Call (720) 492-4326.

Links to other sites

This site may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
