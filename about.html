About Mile High Custom Remodels

Denver's trusted home remodeling company since 2009. We're committed to transforming houses into dream homes with quality craftsmanship, transparent communication, and exceptional customer service.

Our Story

Mile High Custom Remodels was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: to bring quality home remodeling services to Denver homeowners at fair prices with honest, transparent communication.

What started as a small team of three dedicated craftsmen has grown into a full-service remodeling company serving the entire Denver Metro Area. Over the past 15+ years, we've completed more than 500 projects—from quick bathroom updates to complete whole-home transformations.

Our growth has been built on referrals from satisfied customers. We believe in earning every recommendation through quality workmanship, on-time delivery, and the kind of customer service that turns homeowners into lifelong advocates.

Today, Mile High Custom Remodels remains a locally-owned and operated company. We're your neighbors, and we take pride in helping fellow Denver residents create homes they love.
