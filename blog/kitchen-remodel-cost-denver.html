Kitchen Remodel Cost in Denver: Complete 2026 Price Guide

February 15, 2026 12 min read

Planning a kitchen remodel in Denver? Understanding costs upfront helps you budget effectively and avoid surprises. In this comprehensive guide, we break down exactly what you can expect to pay for kitchen renovations in the Denver Metro Area in 2026, from budget-friendly updates to luxury transformations.

Quick Answer: Denver Kitchen Remodel Costs

  • Minor Refresh: $15,000 - $25,000
  • Mid-Range Remodel: $35,000 - $75,000
  • Major Renovation: $75,000 - $150,000
  • Luxury/Custom: $150,000+

These ranges reflect 2026 Denver market rates including materials and labor.

Kitchen Remodel Cost by Project Type

Minor Kitchen Refresh: $15,000 - $25,000

A minor kitchen refresh updates the look without changing the layout. This is ideal for homeowners who like their kitchen's functionality but want a modern appearance.

What's included:

  • Cabinet refacing or painting
  • New hardware and fixtures
  • Updated lighting
  • Fresh paint and backsplash
  • New faucet and sink (optional)

Timeline: 2-3 weeks

Mid-Range Kitchen Remodel: $35,000 - $75,000

The most common choice for Denver homeowners, a mid-range remodel updates all major components while keeping the existing layout.

What's included:

  • New semi-custom cabinets
  • Quartz or granite countertops
  • New appliances (mid-range brands)
  • Tile backsplash
  • New flooring
  • Updated plumbing fixtures
  • Recessed lighting

Timeline: 6-8 weeks

Major Kitchen Renovation: $75,000 - $150,000

A major renovation includes layout changes, structural modifications, and premium materials.

What's included:

  • Custom or high-end semi-custom cabinets
  • Premium countertops (quartzite, marble)
  • Professional-grade appliances
  • Layout modifications
  • Island addition or expansion
  • Electrical upgrades
  • Custom lighting design

Timeline: 10-16 weeks

Luxury Kitchen Remodel: $150,000+

For Denver's high-end homes, luxury kitchen remodels feature top-tier materials, custom craftsmanship, and smart home integration.

What's included:

  • Fully custom cabinetry
  • Exotic stone or designer materials
  • Chef-grade appliances (Sub-Zero, Wolf, Miele)
  • Complete layout redesign
  • Smart home technology
  • Wine storage or butler's pantry
  • Designer fixtures and finishes

Timeline: 16-24+ weeks

Cabinet Costs in Detail

Cabinets are your biggest investment. Here's what to expect in Denver:

  • Stock Cabinets: $75-$200 per linear foot
  • Semi-Custom Cabinets: $150-$650 per linear foot
  • Custom Cabinets: $500-$1,500+ per linear foot

For a typical 20-linear-foot kitchen, expect to pay $4,000-$12,000 for stock, $6,000-$20,000 for semi-custom, or $15,000-$40,000+ for custom cabinets.

  • Granite: $60-$150 per sq ft installed
  • Marble: $100-$200 per sq ft installed

Factors That Affect Kitchen Remodel Cost in Denver

1. Kitchen Size

Larger kitchens require more materials and labor:

  • Small Kitchen (under 100 sq ft): $15,000 - $45,000
  • Average Kitchen (100-200 sq ft): $25,000 - $75,000
  • Large Kitchen (200+ sq ft): $50,000 - $150,000+

2. Layout Changes

Moving plumbing, electrical, or walls significantly increases costs:

  • Moving a sink: $1,500 - $3,500
  • Adding an island with plumbing: $3,000 - $8,000
  • Removing a wall: $1,500 - $10,000 (load-bearing costs more)
  • Electrical panel upgrade: $1,500 - $3,000

3. Age of Home

Older Denver homes may need updates to meet current building codes:

  • Electrical rewiring: $2,000 - $8,000
  • Plumbing updates: $1,500 - $5,000
  • Asbestos abatement (pre-1980s homes): $2,000 - $10,000

4. Neighborhood & Property Value

In higher-end Denver neighborhoods like Cherry Creek, Wash Park, or Highlands, homeowners typically invest more to match neighborhood standards. Budget 10-15% of home value for a kitchen that maintains property value alignment.

5. Permit Requirements

Denver requires permits for electrical, plumbing, and structural work:

  • Building permits: $200 - $1,000
  • Electrical permit: $100 - $300
  • Plumbing permit: $100 - $300
  • Inspection fees: $100 - $500

Denver Kitchen Remodel Labor Costs

Labor typically accounts for 20-35% of your total kitchen remodel budget. Here are current Denver rates:

Contractor & Trade Rates

  • General Contractor: $50 - $150/hour or 15-25% of project cost
  • Carpenter/Cabinet Installer: $50 - $100/hour
  • Electrician: $75 - $125/hour
  • Plumber: $75 - $150/hour
  • Tile Installer: $40 - $100/hour
  • Painter: $35 - $75/hour
  • Countertop Installer: $40 - $75/hour

💡 Pro Tip: Why Denver Labor Costs Are Higher

Denver's construction market is competitive, with skilled tradespeople in high demand. While labor costs are 10-20% higher than the national average, this reflects the quality of local contractors and Denver's cost of living. Always get 3+ quotes and check references.

Smart Ways to Save on Your Denver Kitchen Remodel

1. Keep the Same Layout

Avoiding plumbing and electrical relocation can save $5,000-$15,000. If your kitchen flows well, focus budget on finishes instead of moving things around.

2. Choose Mid-Range Materials Strategically

Splurge where it matters (countertops, cabinet doors) and save elsewhere (cabinet boxes, hardware). Quartz countertops offer the look of marble without the maintenance.

3. Reface Instead of Replace Cabinets

If your cabinet boxes are solid, refacing saves 30-50% vs. new cabinets while completely transforming the look. Refacing costs $5,000-$12,000 vs. $15,000-$30,000 for new.

4. Time Your Project Right

Denver contractors are busiest spring through fall. Scheduling for late fall or winter (November-February) can yield 10-15% savings on labor.

5. Consider Open Shelving

Replacing some upper cabinets with open shelving reduces costs while creating an airy, modern look. Save $2,000-$5,000 on cabinet costs.

6. Shop Appliance Sales

Major holidays (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Black Friday) offer 20-40% off appliances. Denver's Pacific Sales and Ferguson showrooms often have floor model discounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Denver?

Kitchen remodel costs in Denver typically range from $15,000 for a minor refresh to $150,000+ for a luxury renovation. A mid-range kitchen remodel averages $35,000-$75,000, which includes new cabinets, countertops, appliances, and flooring. Labor costs in Denver average $50-$100 per hour for skilled contractors.

What is the most expensive part of a kitchen remodel?

Cabinetry is typically the most expensive part of a kitchen remodel, accounting for 25-35% of the total budget. Custom cabinets can cost $500-$1,500 per linear foot in Denver. Countertops are the second largest expense, ranging from $50-$200 per square foot depending on material choice.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Denver?

A minor kitchen refresh takes 2-3 weeks. A mid-range remodel typically takes 6-8 weeks. A major renovation with layout changes or structural work can take 10-16 weeks. Denver's permitting process adds 1-3 weeks depending on scope.

Is it worth remodeling a kitchen before selling in Denver?

Kitchen remodels in Denver typically offer 60-80% return on investment. Minor remodels often provide better ROI than major renovations when selling. In Denver's competitive market, an updated kitchen can reduce time on market by 20-30%.

Should I hire a general contractor or manage the project myself?

For projects over $25,000, a general contractor is usually worth the 15-25% fee. They manage subcontractors, pull permits, handle inspections, and keep the project on schedule. DIY management works for simple cosmetic updates but becomes challenging with multiple trades involved.

What permits do I need for a kitchen remodel in Denver?

Denver requires permits for electrical work, plumbing changes, structural modifications, and HVAC alterations. Cosmetic updates like painting, refacing cabinets, or replacing appliances in the same location typically don't require permits. Your contractor should handle permit applications.
