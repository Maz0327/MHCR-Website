Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with us

How quickly can you start my project?

Our typical lead time is 2-4 weeks for major renovations, depending on project scope and current schedule. Smaller handyman projects can often be scheduled within 1-2 weeks. Emergency repairs may be available sooner—just call us to discuss your timeline.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! We provide free in-home consultations and detailed written estimates for all projects. There's no obligation, and we'll thoroughly explain all costs and options so you can make an informed decision.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, personal checks, all major credit cards, and offer financing options for qualified homeowners on larger projects. Payment schedules are typically structured with a deposit, progress payments, and final payment upon completion.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Mile High Custom Remodels is fully licensed in the State of Colorado, carries comprehensive general liability insurance, and maintains workers' compensation coverage for all employees. We're happy to provide proof of insurance upon request.

Do you handle permits?

Absolutely. We handle all permit applications and coordinate required inspections as part of our full-service approach. Proper permitting protects your investment and ensures your renovation meets all building codes.

What warranty do you offer?

We stand behind our work with a workmanship warranty on all projects. Additionally, materials and fixtures come with their respective manufacturer warranties. Specific warranty details are included in your project contract.
