What to Do in a Home Emergency

1

Ensure Safety First

If there's a gas leak, evacuate and call Xcel Energy at 1-800-895-2999. For electrical fires, do not use water. For flooding, avoid standing water near electrical sources.

2

Stop the Source

Know where your main water shut-off valve is located. Turn off water at the source if there's a leak. Turn off breakers if there's an electrical issue.

3

Call Us Immediately

Call (303) 555-0123 and describe the emergency. We'll dispatch a technician and provide guidance while they're en route.

4

Document the Damage

Take photos and videos for insurance purposes. Note the time the issue started and any actions you took. This helps with claims and repairs.

Emergency Repair FAQs

Do you offer same-day emergency repairs in Denver?

Yes, we offer same-day emergency repair service throughout the Denver Metro Area. For urgent issues like water leaks, burst pipes, or safety hazards, call (303) 555-0123 and we'll dispatch a technician as quickly as possible, often within 2-4 hours.

What is considered an emergency home repair?

Emergency home repairs include: active water leaks or flooding, burst pipes, no hot water in winter, electrical hazards (sparking outlets, burning smell), gas leaks (call utility first), broken windows or doors compromising security, and major appliance failures causing damage.

How much do emergency repairs cost in Denver?

Emergency repair service calls start at $150-$250 for the first hour, with additional time billed at $75-$150/hour depending on the type of work. After-hours or weekend emergencies may have a premium. We provide upfront pricing before beginning any work.

What areas do you serve for emergency repairs?

We provide emergency services throughout the Denver Metro Area including Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Littleton, Parker, and Golden. Response times may be longer for locations outside the immediate metro area.

Can you help with insurance claims?

Yes, we document all emergency work with photos and detailed invoices suitable for insurance claims. We can work directly with your insurance adjuster if needed and provide estimates for full restoration work.

What if the emergency happens outside business hours?

We offer after-hours emergency service for urgent situations. There is an additional $50-$100 premium for evenings, weekends, and holidays. Call our main number and follow the prompts for emergency service.
