Kitchen Remodeling

Your questions about Denver kitchen renovations answered

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Denver?

Kitchen remodeling costs in Denver typically range from $25,000 to $100,000 or more depending on scope and finishes:

  • Basic refresh (stock cabinets, laminate countertops, new fixtures): $25,000–$40,000
  • Mid-range renovation (semi-custom cabinets, quartz countertops, new appliances, updated flooring): $40,000–$70,000
  • Luxury remodel (custom cabinetry, premium stone, high-end appliances, structural changes): $70,000–$100,000+

These figures reflect 2026 Denver Metro market rates. Mile High Custom Remodels provides free estimates tailored to your specific kitchen and goals.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A typical kitchen remodel in Denver takes 6 to 12 weeks from demolition to final walk-through. More specifically:

  • Cosmetic updates (cabinet refacing, countertops, backsplash only): 2–4 weeks
  • Full renovation (new cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, electrical): 8–12 weeks
  • Complex projects (structural changes, custom fabrication, wall removal): 12–16 weeks

Lead time for custom cabinetry and specialty stone can add several weeks before on-site work begins. We provide a detailed schedule at the start of every project.

Do I need to move out during a kitchen remodel?

Most homeowners stay in their homes during a kitchen remodel. We take several steps to keep your daily life manageable:

  • Set up a temporary kitchen with microwave, toaster oven, coffee maker, and mini refrigerator in another room
  • Install plastic dust barriers to contain demolition debris
  • Conduct thorough daily cleanup at the end of each workday
  • Schedule the most disruptive work (demolition, rough-in) into the first 1–2 weeks

If your home has only one bathroom or the remodel is especially large, you may prefer temporary housing during the initial demolition phase. We will discuss this with you before work begins.

What's included in a kitchen remodel?

A full kitchen remodel from Mile High Custom Remodels typically includes:

  • Demolition and disposal of existing materials
  • Custom or semi-custom cabinet design and installation
  • Countertop fabrication and installation (quartz, granite, marble, or laminate)
  • Backsplash tile design and installation
  • Flooring installation (tile, hardwood, or luxury vinyl plank)
  • Lighting upgrades including recessed lights and under-cabinet lighting
  • Appliance delivery and installation
  • Plumbing rough-in and fixture installation (sink, faucet, dishwasher)
  • Electrical work (new circuits, GFCI outlets, range hood venting)
  • Painting and trim
  • All permit applications and inspection coordination

A dedicated project manager oversees every phase from design through final punch list. We can also assist with structural work such as wall removal or window additions when required.

Bathroom Renovation

Common questions about Denver bathroom remodeling

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Denver?

Bathroom renovation costs in Denver vary by scope and bathroom size:

  • Half bath / powder room (toilet, vanity, flooring, fixtures): $8,000–$15,000
  • Full bathroom update (new tub/shower, tile, vanity, toilet): $15,000–$25,000
  • Master bathroom renovation (custom walk-in shower, freestanding tub, double vanity, heated floors): $25,000–$50,000+

Premium materials — imported tile, custom glass enclosures, steam showers — can push costs higher. We provide itemized estimates so you understand exactly what you are paying for.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

Bathroom renovation timelines depend on the scope of the project:

  • Powder room update: 1–2 weeks
  • Standard full bathroom remodel: 3–4 weeks
  • Full master bathroom renovation: 4–6 weeks
  • Projects with custom tile, structural changes, or specialty materials: 6–8+ weeks

Key inspections during rough-in plumbing and electrical must pass before walls are closed — this is a required step that adds a few days to the schedule but ensures code compliance. We provide a detailed project schedule at the start of every bathroom job.

Can you add a bathroom to my home?

Yes. Mile High Custom Remodels regularly adds bathrooms to existing homes — converting a closet to a powder room, adding a full bath to a finished basement, or building an en-suite master bath from an adjacent bedroom.

Adding a bathroom requires plumbing rough-in (supply and drain lines), electrical (GFCI outlets, lighting, exhaust fan), framing, drywall, tile, and fixtures. Typical costs:

  • New half bath addition: Starting around $15,000–$20,000
  • New full bathroom addition: $25,000–$50,000+ depending on finishes

Permits are required and we handle the entire process. Contact us for a free estimate.

What's the difference between a bathroom update and a full renovation?

A bathroom update is cosmetic — replacing a vanity, re-grouting tile, painting, swapping a light fixture, and installing a new toilet without moving any plumbing. Updates are faster (1–2 weeks) and less expensive ($5,000–$15,000).

A full bathroom renovation involves demolition down to the studs, plumbing relocation, custom shower construction, new tile throughout, new electrical, heated floors, and completely new fixtures. Full renovations take 3–6 weeks and cost $15,000–$50,000+.

We help you assess which approach makes sense based on your goals, budget, and the existing condition of your bathroom. Often, a targeted update delivers excellent results at a fraction of the cost of a full renovation.

Basement Finishing

Answers to common basement finishing questions for Denver homeowners

How much does basement finishing cost in Denver?

Basement finishing costs in Denver depend on the square footage and what spaces you are creating:

  • Basic finish (drywall, flooring, paint, basic lighting): $30,000–$45,000
  • Mid-range buildout (bedroom, bathroom, open living area): $45,000–$60,000
  • Premium buildout (full guest suite, home theater, wet bar, gym, high-end finishes): $60,000–$75,000+

Denver's high altitude and variable climate affect decisions about moisture control, insulation, and HVAC — all of which factor into our estimates. We conduct a thorough assessment of your unfinished basement before quoting to identify any moisture, radon, or structural considerations that need to be addressed first.

How long does it take to finish a basement?

Finishing a basement in Denver typically takes 6–10 weeks for a standard buildout:

  • Open-plan basement with bathroom and bedroom: 6–8 weeks
  • Complex multi-room basement (theater, wet bar, custom built-ins): 10–14 weeks

Key phases include framing, rough-in plumbing and electrical (which require city inspection before walls are closed), insulation, drywall, flooring, trim, painting, and fixture installation. We coordinate all inspections and keep you informed at each milestone.

Do I need a permit to finish my basement in Colorado?

Yes — a building permit is required to finish a basement in Colorado in virtually all cases. Basement finishing involves:

  • Framing (requires framing inspection)
  • Electrical work (requires rough-in and final electrical inspection)
  • Plumbing, if adding a bathroom (rough-in and final plumbing inspections)
  • HVAC modifications (mechanical inspection)
  • Egress window installation for bedrooms (required by code for legal sleeping rooms)

Unpermitted basement work creates serious problems at resale, may not be covered by homeowner's insurance, and can pose genuine safety risks. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspection coordination for every basement finishing project.

What can I do with a finished basement?

A finished basement dramatically expands your usable living space. Popular uses include:

  • Family room or recreation room for movie nights and entertaining
  • Dedicated home office or study
  • Guest bedroom suite with full bathroom
  • Home gym or yoga studio
  • Children's playroom
  • Home theater with surround sound and projection screen
  • Wet bar or game room
  • Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) / rental unit (where permitted by municipality)

Many Denver homeowners combine multiple uses — for example, a guest suite plus a home gym plus a home office — in one finished basement buildout. Our project managers can help you plan the layout to maximize the space.

Yes. Mile High Custom Remodels offers emergency repair services for urgent situations throughout the Denver Metro Area. Common emergencies we handle include:

Do you do whole home renovations?

Yes. Whole home renovations are one of our specialties at Mile High Custom Remodels. These projects typically involve remodeling multiple rooms simultaneously — kitchen, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms, and often the basement — along with system-level upgrades to electrical, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, and windows.

Coordinating all trades under a single project manager produces better results: fewer scheduling conflicts, better communication, and a cohesive final design across the whole home.

  • Partial whole-home renovation (3–5 rooms): $75,000–$150,000
  • Full property transformation: $150,000–$300,000+

We provide a comprehensive project plan, timeline, and fixed-price contract before any work begins. Contact us for a free consultation.

Can you match existing materials and finishes?

We make every effort to match existing materials and finishes for repairs and partial renovations:

  • Hardwood floors: We source matching species and stain to blend repairs seamlessly. Keeping leftover flooring from the original installation helps greatly.
  • Tile: We recommend keeping spare boxes from original installations. For discontinued tile, we source the closest available match.
  • Paint: We color-match using paint chip samples or a spectrophotometer for an accurate blend.
  • Trim and moldings: We source matching profiles from millwork suppliers or have them custom-milled when necessary.

We are transparent when an exact match is not achievable and will discuss alternatives that blend as naturally as possible. Sometimes a thoughtful contrast — a complementary but deliberately different finish — is the best design decision.
