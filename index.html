FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Remodeling in Denver

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Denver?

Kitchen remodeling costs in Denver typically range from $25,000 to $100,000+ depending on the scope and finishes. A basic kitchen remodel with cabinet refacing, new countertops, and updated fixtures averages $25,000-$40,000. A mid-range renovation with new cabinets, stone countertops, and appliance upgrades runs $40,000-$70,000. Luxury kitchen remodels with custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, and premium materials can exceed $100,000. Mile High Custom Remodels provides free detailed estimates for all kitchen projects.

How long does a bathroom renovation take in Denver?

Bathroom renovation timelines in Denver typically range from 2-6 weeks depending on the project scope. A basic bathroom update with new fixtures, vanity, and tile takes approximately 2-3 weeks. A full master bathroom renovation with layout changes, custom tile work, and new plumbing averages 4-6 weeks. Our project managers provide detailed timelines during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout construction.

Do I need a permit for home remodeling in Denver?

Permit requirements for home remodeling in Denver depend on the scope of work. Structural changes, electrical work, plumbing modifications, and additions typically require permits from the City of Denver. Cosmetic updates like painting, flooring replacement, and cabinet installation usually don't need permits. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections as part of our full-service approach, ensuring your project meets all local building codes.
