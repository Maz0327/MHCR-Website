Denver Remodeling FAQs

Common questions about home remodeling in Denver

Kitchen remodeling in Denver typically costs $25,000-$75,000 for a mid-range renovation and $75,000-$150,000+ for high-end remodels. Costs vary based on kitchen size, material selections, and scope of work. Denver's housing market often sees good ROI on kitchen updates—typically 60-80% return on investment.

Denver requires building permits for most remodeling work involving structural changes, electrical, plumbing, or mechanical systems. The City and County of Denver Development Services issues permits. Cosmetic updates like painting and flooring typically don't require permits. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections for our Denver projects.
