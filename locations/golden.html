Home Remodeling in Golden, Colorado

Sensitive renovations for a historic foothills town, from Victorian cottages to hillside homes

Founded in 1859 and once Colorado's territorial capital, Golden carries more history in its housing than almost any nearby city. Downtown holds Victorian and early-1900s homes with real character, the areas around the Table Mountains mix mid-century and newer construction, the slopes near Lookout Mountain hold custom hillside homes, and the Applewood area offers large-lot postwar ranches.

Golden's Distinctive Housing

The right approach depends on the home's age and setting

Historic Downtown Homes

The Victorian-era and early-1900s houses near downtown were built with plaster walls, narrow framing, and dated wiring. Renovating them well means updating systems while protecting the millwork and proportions that define them.

Table Mountain Neighborhoods

The areas below North and South Table Mountain blend mid-century homes with newer infill, giving us room to modernize floor plans and finishes without the most historic constraints.

Hillside Homes near Lookout Mountain

Custom homes on the foothills slopes come with stepped foundations, rocky ground, and dramatic views. We design around the terrain, prioritizing drainage, access, and window placement.

Applewood Ranches

The postwar ranches in the Applewood area sit on large, tree-lined lots. Their simple structures welcome open kitchens, suite additions, and basement finishes that use the generous land.

Remodeling Considerations Unique to Golden

Golden differs from its flatland neighbors in ways that shape a remodel. Foothills lots bring rockier soils, slope, and drainage questions, and homes in the wildland interface benefit from fire-conscious materials on anything near the exterior. The city building department, not the county, reviews work inside city limits.

Older homes carry lead paint, asbestos-era materials, and sometimes settling foundations worth inspecting first. When a historic renovation needs finish repairs and punch-list fixes, our handyman services handle the details, and homeowners east of the mesas can explore our Lakewood remodeling services.
