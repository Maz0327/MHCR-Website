Lakewood Home Expertise

We understand the unique characteristics of Lakewood homes

Mid-Century Ranches

The brick ranches built in Lakewood's 1950s-1970s development era are iconic. We specialize in opening up their floor plans, updating kitchens and bathrooms, and modernizing systems while honoring their classic charm.

Split-Level Homes

Lakewood has many split-level homes that can be transformed with strategic updates. We can improve flow between levels, update dated finishes, and create better connections to outdoor spaces.

Bi-Level Homes

Bi-level homes in areas like Green Mountain offer great remodeling potential. Finishing the lower level, updating the main floor, or adding outdoor living spaces are popular improvements.

Newer Construction

Lakewood's newer developments like Belmar feature more contemporary homes. We help homeowners customize builder-grade finishes and add personalized touches.
