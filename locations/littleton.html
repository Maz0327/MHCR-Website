The Homes That Make Up Littleton

Each era of Littleton housing asks for its own approach

Historic Main Street Homes

The late-1800s and early-1900s houses near downtown often sit within the historic district. We concentrate on interior modernization and careful system upgrades that protect their exterior character.

Mid-Century Ranches

Columbine, Heritage Village, and similar neighborhoods are full of 1950s-to-1970s ranches with low basements and closed floor plans that reward opening the main level and finishing the lower level.

Ken Caryl Planned Community

The 1970s and 1980s homes of Ken Caryl sit against the hogback under active HOA guidelines. Renovations here balance design-review standards with the drainage hillside-adjacent lots demand.

Newer Homes at Grant Ranch and Wilder

The 1990s and 2000s houses in Littleton's newer pockets were built with standardized finishes. Owners personalize them with upgraded cabinetry, flooring, and primary suites.
