Home Remodeling in Thornton, Colorado

Kitchen, bath, and basement renovations for one of Colorado's original planned suburbs

Incorporated in 1956, Thornton was one of Colorado's first master-planned postwar suburbs, and its housing tells that story. The original core holds compact 1950s-to-1970s ranches, the middle rings added two-story tract homes through the 1990s, and the northern edge keeps filling in with new construction. We tailor every project to the era your Thornton home belongs to.

Understanding Thornton's Housing Stock

Our approach changes with the decade your home was built

Original-Core Ranches (1950s-1970s)

The single-story homes around Original Thornton were built small and compartmentalized. They reward layout changes, ceiling updates that remove dated textures, and refreshed electrical service for modern appliances.

Tract Two-Stories (1980s-1990s)

Neighborhoods like Hunters Glen filled in with solid two-story production homes. Owners typically want the walled-off floor plans opened up and the oak-and-laminate finishes replaced.

New Construction (2000s-2010s)

North Thornton's newer sections offer contemporary layouts with builder-grade finishes. We help owners personalize them with upgraded cabinetry, trim, and built-ins.

Large-Lot Homes at Todd Creek

The acreage and golf-course lots at Todd Creek support larger kitchens, primary-suite expansions, and finished lower levels that use the extra room.

What to Know Before Remodeling in Thornton

Thornton sits on the expansive clay soils common across the northern metro, so any project touching the foundation or adding a basement egress well should account for how those soils move with moisture. The city also lies in a known Front Range hail corridor.

Older Thornton homes are strong candidates for a living room remodel that ties a newly opened kitchen into the main living space. Homeowners just west of here can also explore our Arvada remodeling services.
