Home Remodeling in Westminster, Colorado

Renovations for a city that stretches from postwar ranches to brand-new downtown townhomes

Sitting midway between Denver and Boulder and straddling the Adams and Jefferson county line, Westminster holds an unusually wide range of housing. The original core near 72nd and Federal is anchored by 1950s and 1960s ranches, the 1970s through 1990s brought split-levels and two-stories, and recent decades added Legacy Ridge, Bradburn, and the ground-up Downtown Westminster district.

Westminster Homes We Know Well

Different eras call for different remodeling strategies

Postwar Ranches (1950s-1960s)

The oldest neighborhoods near the historic core feature plaster-walled ranches with tight kitchens and original systems. They benefit from layout openings paired with insulation and window upgrades.

Split-Levels and Two-Stories (1970s-1990s)

Westminster's mid-era expansion produced a large stock of split-levels and two-story homes, ideal candidates for opening the main level and finishing the lower level into usable rooms.

Golf-Community Homes at Legacy Ridge

The 1990s and 2000s homes around Legacy Ridge have generous footprints and strong bones. Owners often pursue kitchen expansions and primary-suite upgrades to match the neighborhood.

New Urbanism at Bradburn and Downtown

Bradburn's walkable early-2000s homes and the newer Downtown Westminster townhomes were built recently but standardized. We personalize finishes and storage within their tighter footprints.

Planning a Remodel in Westminster

Because Westminster is split between Adams and Jefferson counties, buyers sometimes wonder which jurisdiction governs their project. In practice the city building division handles permitting either way, and we manage that process end to end.
