Our Basement Finishing Process

From concept to completion, we handle everything

1

Free Consultation

We evaluate your basement, discuss your vision, identify any moisture or structural issues, and determine the best approach for your space and budget.

2

Design & Planning

Our team creates a detailed floor plan and design that maximizes your basement's potential. We handle all permit applications with the City of Denver.

3

Moisture Mitigation

If needed, we address any moisture issues before construction. A dry basement is essential for a successful finish. We install proper waterproofing and drainage systems.

4

Framing & Rough-In

We frame walls, install egress windows if needed, and complete rough-in work for electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems. Inspections are scheduled as required.

5

Insulation & Drywall

Proper insulation is installed for comfort and energy efficiency, followed by drywall, taping, and finishing for smooth, painted walls.

6

Finishes & Fixtures

We install flooring, trim, doors, lighting fixtures, bathroom fixtures, and all finishing touches that bring your basement to life.

7

Final Inspection & Walkthrough

After passing final inspections, we conduct a detailed walkthrough with you to ensure every detail meets our quality standards and your expectations.

Basement Finishing FAQs

Common questions about Denver basement remodeling

Basement finishing costs in Denver typically range from $25 to $65 per square foot, with most projects totaling between $30,000 and $75,000 or more. For a typical 1,000 square foot basement, expect to pay $25,000-$35,000 for a basic finish, $40,000-$55,000 for a mid-range finish with bathroom, or $55,000-$100,000+ for a high-end finish with multiple rooms, bathroom, wet bar, and premium finishes.

A typical basement finishing project takes 4-8 weeks to complete. A basic finish with one large open space takes 4-5 weeks. A mid-range project with multiple rooms and a bathroom takes 6-8 weeks. Complex projects with custom features, wet bars, or extensive electrical/plumbing work may take 8-12 weeks. Timeline depends on permits, material availability, and project complexity.

Yes, permits are required to finish a basement in Denver. You'll need a building permit for framing and drywall, an electrical permit for wiring and fixtures, a plumbing permit if adding a bathroom or wet bar, and a mechanical permit for HVAC work. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections as part of our service.

An egress window is a window large enough for emergency escape, required by code in any basement bedroom. In Denver, egress windows must have a minimum opening of 5.7 square feet, be at least 24 inches high and 20 inches wide, and have a sill no higher than 44 inches from the floor. If you're adding a basement bedroom, egress window installation is required. Cost is typically $2,500-$5,000.

Yes, finishing a basement adds significant value to your Denver home. You can expect to recoup 70-75% of your investment at resale. A finished basement adds usable square footage, which directly increases home value. In the Denver market, finished basements are highly desirable and can make your home sell faster. A well-finished basement with a bedroom and bathroom adds the most value.

Yes, you can add a bathroom to your basement. Many Denver homes have rough-in plumbing already in place, which reduces costs. If no rough-in exists, we can install plumbing using traditional methods or an up-flush system. A basement bathroom typically costs $8,000-$20,000 depending on size and finishes. Adding a bathroom significantly increases the utility and value of your finished basement.

Moisture management is critical for any basement finishing project. During our consultation, we assess your basement for moisture issues. If present, we recommend solutions such as interior drainage systems, sump pumps, waterproofing membranes, or exterior waterproofing. We won't begin a basement finish until moisture issues are resolved, ensuring your investment is protected long-term.
