Bathroom Remodeling FAQs

Common questions about bathroom renovation in Denver

Bathroom remodeling costs in Denver typically range from $10,000 to $50,000 or more, depending on the size and scope. A basic guest bathroom remodel averages $10,000-$15,000. A mid-range primary bathroom renovation costs $15,000-$30,000. A luxury master bathroom remodel with high-end finishes can exceed $50,000. The average bathroom remodel in Denver costs around $20,000-$25,000.

A typical bathroom renovation takes 2-4 weeks to complete. A simple cosmetic update (new vanity, fixtures, paint) can be finished in 1-2 weeks. A complete gut renovation with layout changes takes 3-4 weeks. Factors affecting timeline include permit requirements, custom orders, and the extent of plumbing or electrical work needed.

In Denver, permits are typically required for bathroom remodels that involve plumbing changes, electrical work, or structural modifications. Cosmetic updates like painting, replacing fixtures, or installing a new vanity usually don't require permits. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections as part of our service.

The best bathroom floor tiles include porcelain tile (most durable, water-resistant, low maintenance), ceramic tile (affordable, wide variety of styles), natural stone (marble, slate, travertine for luxury look), and vinyl tile (budget-friendly, waterproof). For Denver homes, we recommend porcelain or ceramic tile for durability and ease of maintenance.

The choice between a bathtub and walk-in shower depends on your needs. Walk-in showers are ideal for accessibility, modern aesthetics, and homes with limited space. Bathtubs are preferred for families with children, relaxation (soaking tubs), and maintaining home resale value. Many Denver homeowners opt for a walk-in shower in the master bath and keep a tub in a secondary bathroom.

The bathroom features that add the most value include: double vanities (especially in master baths), walk-in showers with frameless glass, updated lighting and ventilation, new tile flooring, and modern fixtures. In Denver, bathroom remodels typically return 60-70% of the investment at resale. Focus on quality materials and timeless design over trendy features.
