Bedroom Renovation Cost in Denver

Understanding what to budget for your bedroom project

Bedroom renovation costs in Denver vary widely depending on the scope of work. Simple updates like flooring and paint are affordable, while full master suite additions are a major investment. Here's what Denver homeowners typically spend:

Cosmetic Refresh

$3,000 – $12,000

Ideal for rooms in good condition needing an update:

  • New flooring (carpet or LVP)
  • Fresh paint and trim
  • Lighting fixture replacements
  • Closet organization systems
  • Door and hardware upgrades

Full Bedroom Renovation

$12,000 – $35,000

A complete transformation with quality materials:

  • Custom closet system
  • Hardwood or luxury vinyl flooring
  • Accent wall (board & batten, shiplap, or wood panel)
  • Recessed lighting and dimmer controls
  • Crown molding and upgraded trim
  • Window upgrades

Master Suite Addition

$35,000 – $100,000+

Create the master retreat you've always wanted:

  • Room addition or conversion
  • En-suite bathroom renovation
  • Walk-in closet with built-ins
  • Vaulted or tray ceiling
  • Premium flooring throughout
  • Smart home integration

Factors that affect cost: Room size, closet scope, flooring material, ceiling height modifications, en-suite bathroom additions, and the quality of fixtures and finishes all influence your final investment.

Bedroom Renovation FAQs

Answers to common questions about bedroom renovations in Denver

How much does a bedroom renovation cost in Denver?

A basic bedroom refresh—new flooring, paint, and lighting—typically costs $3,000–$12,000. A full renovation including custom closets, accent walls, crown molding, and new flooring averages $12,000–$35,000. A master suite addition with an en-suite bathroom can run $35,000–$100,000 or more depending on size and finishes. Mile High Custom Remodels provides detailed, itemized quotes at no charge.

How long does a bedroom renovation take?

A cosmetic update (paint, flooring, lighting) can be completed in 1–2 weeks. A full renovation with closet systems, accent walls, and trim work typically takes 2–4 weeks. Master suite additions involving structural changes, new bathrooms, or room additions take 6–12 weeks. We provide a written schedule before work begins so you know exactly what to expect each day.

What is the best flooring for a bedroom?

The most popular bedroom flooring choices in Denver are carpet, hardwood, and luxury vinyl plank (LVP). Carpet provides warmth, softness underfoot, and sound dampening—ideal for primary bedrooms. Hardwood adds elegance and lasting value. LVP is a durable, moisture-resistant, and cost-effective option that mimics hardwood beautifully. We help you choose the right material for your lifestyle, budget, and design goals.

Do I need permits for a bedroom renovation?

Most cosmetic bedroom renovations—flooring, paint, trim, and lighting fixture swaps—don't require permits in Denver. Permits are needed when adding new electrical circuits, moving walls, adding windows or egress requirements, or building room additions. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections when required.

Can I stay in my home during the renovation?

Yes. Most homeowners remain in their home during a bedroom renovation. We use dust containment barriers, clean up thoroughly every day, and work efficiently to restore access to the room as quickly as possible. For larger master suite additions, you may need to temporarily use another bedroom, but we'll plan the project around your household's needs.

How do I create a master suite from a standard bedroom?

Creating a master suite typically involves three main upgrades: adding or expanding an en-suite bathroom, creating a walk-in closet (often by converting an adjacent room or closet space), and enhancing the bedroom's finishes with premium flooring, lighting, and architectural details. Some projects also involve minor structural changes to create an open, cohesive layout. We assess your existing space and design the most efficient path to your dream master suite.
