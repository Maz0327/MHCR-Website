Our Services

Bedroom Renovation Services

From closet organization to complete room additions, we handle every aspect of your bedroom transformation.

Custom Closet Systems

Maximize your storage with custom-designed closet organization systems featuring adjustable shelving, drawers, hanging rods, and specialized storage for shoes, accessories, and more.

Flooring Installation

Upgrade your bedroom floors with warm hardwood, cozy carpet, or modern luxury vinyl plank. We help you choose the perfect material for comfort and style.

Accent Walls

Create visual interest with statement accent walls featuring wood panels, shiplap, textured wallpaper, board and batten, or stunning paint treatments.

Lighting Upgrades

Improve your bedroom's ambiance with layered lighting including recessed lights, bedside sconces, ceiling fans with lights, and smart dimmer controls.

Crown Molding & Trim

Add elegance and architectural detail with crown molding, baseboard upgrades, window casings, and decorative trim work that elevates your space.

Room Additions

Need more space? We build bedroom additions and convert existing spaces like attics or bonus rooms into comfortable new bedrooms with proper insulation and egress.
