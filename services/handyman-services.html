Get the Most from Your Handyman Visit

Tips to maximize value and efficiency

1

Make a List

Gather all your small repairs into one visit. Fixing 5 things in one trip is much more cost-effective than 5 separate service calls.

2

Prioritize Your List

Rank items by importance. If time runs short, you'll know the most critical repairs get done first.

3

Take Photos

When scheduling, send us photos of the repairs needed. This helps us bring the right tools and materials on the first trip.

4

Clear the Area

Move furniture and clear clutter from work areas before we arrive. Less prep time means more repair time.
