Services/Kitchen

Kitchen remodeling

Cabinet painting, crown molding, stone, lighting and floors, up to a full remodel.

Not every kitchen needs a tear-out.

Painting the cabinets you have, adding crown molding and cutting a half wall down can change the room.

When it needs more than that, we do the full remodel. Either way, we'll tell you what's worth doing and what isn't.

The stone and trim work is custom, cut and fit by hand on site.

What we do

  • Cabinet painting, crown molding and under-cabinet lights
  • Half walls cut down and counters extended
  • Stone work, like a stone-wrapped peninsula
  • Hardwood and tile floors
  • Full kitchen remodels
