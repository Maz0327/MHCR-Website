Understanding what to expect for your budget

Kitchen remodeling costs in Denver vary based on the scope of work, materials selected, and complexity of the project. Here's what Denver homeowners typically invest in their kitchen renovations:

Basic Kitchen Remodel

$25,000 - $40,000

Ideal for kitchens that are structurally sound but need cosmetic updates. Includes:

  • Stock or semi-stock cabinets
  • Laminate or entry-level quartz countertops
  • Basic tile backsplash
  • New fixtures and hardware
  • Paint and minor repairs

Mid-Range Kitchen Remodel

$40,000 - $70,000

A complete transformation with quality materials and custom touches:

  • Semi-custom cabinetry with soft-close
  • Quartz or granite countertops
  • Designer tile backsplash

  • Updated lighting package
  • New appliances (mid-range brands)

Luxury Kitchen Remodel

$75,000 - $150,000+

Dream kitchen with premium everything and custom craftsmanship:

  • Fully custom cabinetry

Factors that affect cost: Kitchen size, layout changes, structural modifications, appliance selections, countertop material and edge profiles, cabinet construction quality, and flooring choice all impact your final investment.

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

Answers to common questions about kitchen renovation in Denver

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Denver?

Kitchen remodeling costs in Denver typically range from $25,000 to $100,000 or more, depending on the scope and finishes. A basic kitchen remodel with stock cabinets and laminate countertops averages $25,000-$40,000. A mid-range renovation with semi-custom cabinets and quartz countertops costs $40,000-$70,000. A luxury kitchen remodel with custom cabinetry, premium countertops, and high-end appliances can exceed $100,000.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A typical kitchen remodel in Denver takes 6 to 12 weeks from demolition to completion. Simple cosmetic updates like refacing cabinets and installing new countertops can be completed in 2-4 weeks. A full kitchen renovation with layout changes, new cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, and electrical work takes 8-12 weeks. Complex projects with structural changes or custom fabrication may take 12-16 weeks.

What is the best countertop material for kitchens?

The best countertop material depends on your priorities. Quartz is the most popular choice in Denver kitchens due to its durability, low maintenance, and wide range of colors. Granite offers natural beauty and heat resistance. Marble provides a luxurious look but requires more maintenance. Butcher block adds warmth but needs regular sealing. For budget-friendly options, laminate and solid surface materials have improved significantly in appearance and durability.

Should I reface or replace my kitchen cabinets?

Cabinet refacing is ideal if your existing cabinet boxes are structurally sound and you're satisfied with your current kitchen layout. Refacing costs 40-50% less than full replacement. You should replace cabinets if they're damaged, poorly constructed, or if you want to change your kitchen layout. New cabinets also allow you to add modern features like soft-close hinges, pull-out organizers, and better storage solutions.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in Denver?

Permits are required in Denver for kitchen remodels involving structural changes (wall removal, window additions), electrical work (new circuits, panel upgrades), plumbing changes (moving sinks, adding gas lines), or mechanical work. Cosmetic updates like painting, replacing countertops, or installing new appliances typically don't require permits. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications and inspections for our clients.

Can I live in my home during a kitchen remodel?

Yes, most homeowners stay in their homes during a kitchen remodel. We help you set up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, toaster oven, and coffee maker in another room. We contain dust and debris with plastic barriers, and our crews clean up daily. The most disruptive period is typically the first two weeks during demolition and rough work. We do everything possible to minimize inconvenience.

What is the ROI on a kitchen remodel?

According to Remodeling Magazine's Cost vs. Value Report, a mid-range kitchen remodel in the Mountain region recoups approximately 50-75% of costs at resale. Minor kitchen remodels (under $30,000) often have the highest ROI. Beyond resale value, a kitchen remodel improves your daily quality of life, makes cooking and entertaining more enjoyable, and increases your home's appeal if you decide to sell.

How do I choose a kitchen remodeling contractor in Denver?

Look for a contractor who is licensed, insured, and has strong local references. Check online reviews on Google, Yelp, and Houzz. Ask to see a portfolio of completed projects similar to yours. Get multiple bids but don't automatically choose the cheapest—quality and communication matter. Ensure they pull proper permits and offer a written warranty.
