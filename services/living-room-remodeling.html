Our Services

Living Room Remodeling Services

From minor updates to complete transformations, we handle every aspect of your living room renovation.

Custom Built-Ins

Beautiful custom built-in shelving, entertainment centers, and storage solutions designed to maximize your space and complement your home's architecture.

Flooring Installation

Premium hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, tile, and carpet installation. We help you choose the perfect flooring to match your lifestyle and design vision.

Fireplace Remodeling

Transform your fireplace into a stunning focal point with stone veneer, tile surrounds, custom mantels, and modern gas insert conversions.

Open Concept Conversions

Remove walls to create an open floor plan that connects your living room, kitchen, and dining areas for a modern, spacious feel.

Lighting Design

Layered lighting solutions including recessed lighting, pendant fixtures, sconces, and dimmer switches to create the perfect ambiance.

Accent Walls & Trim

Statement accent walls, wainscoting, crown molding, and architectural trim work that adds character and elegance to your living space.
