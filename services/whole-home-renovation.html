Why Choose Whole Home Renovation?

The benefits of a comprehensive approach

Cohesive Design

When renovating the whole home at once, every element works together—consistent flooring, coordinated colors, unified fixtures, and seamless flow between rooms.

Cost Efficiency

Renovating everything at once is typically 15-25% more cost-effective than doing multiple separate projects over time. One mobilization, one management team, bulk purchasing.

Single Disruption

Instead of living through multiple renovation projects over several years, complete your home transformation in one focused period and enjoy the results sooner.

Maximum Value

A comprehensive renovation adds more value than the sum of individual projects. Buyers pay premium prices for move-in-ready, fully updated homes.

Whole Home Renovation FAQs

Common questions about complete home remodeling

How much does a whole home renovation cost in Denver?

Whole home renovation costs in Denver typically range from $100,000 to $500,000 or more, depending on the home's size, scope of work, and finish level. A moderate whole home renovation averages $100-$200 per square foot. For a 2,000 sq ft home, expect $150,000-$300,000 for a comprehensive renovation. High-end renovations with luxury finishes can exceed $300 per square foot.

How long does a whole home renovation take?

A typical whole home renovation takes 3-6 months to complete. Smaller projects with minimal structural changes may take 2-3 months. Large-scale renovations with additions, structural modifications, or extensive custom work can take 6-12 months. Timeline depends on project scope, permit requirements, and material lead times.

Can I live in my home during a whole house renovation?

Whether you can live in your home during renovation depends on the scope of work. For partial renovations completed in phases, living at home may be possible. For extensive renovations involving electrical, plumbing, or structural work throughout the home, temporary relocation is usually recommended for 2-4 months. We can help plan the project to minimize displacement.

Do I need permits for a whole home renovation?

Yes, whole home renovations in Denver require multiple permits including building permits for structural work, electrical permits, plumbing permits, and mechanical permits. If changing the home's footprint or adding square footage, additional approvals may be needed. Mile High Custom Remodels handles all permit applications, inspections, and compliance as part of our service.

Is it cheaper to renovate or buy a new house?

In Denver's housing market, renovating is often more cost-effective than buying new—especially if you love your location. Renovation lets you customize everything to your preferences while potentially avoiding moving costs, realtor fees, and higher property taxes from reassessment. A whole home renovation typically costs 30-50% less than purchasing an equivalent updated home in the same neighborhood.

How do you handle unexpected issues during renovation?

Surprises are common in renovation, especially in older homes. We recommend budgeting a 10-15% contingency for unexpected issues. When discoveries occur (outdated wiring, hidden water damage, structural issues), we document everything, present options, and get your approval before proceeding. Our experience helps us anticipate and plan for common issues during the initial assessment.
